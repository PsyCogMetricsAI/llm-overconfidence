The MIT license applies to original project software and new reproduction adapters. It does not relicense third-party libraries, benchmark/evaluation data, cited papers, manuscript publication rights, or upstream source material. Preserve each dependency's applicable license and attribution.

Upstream dataset records and authoritative metadata are collected under upstream_metadata. Some evaluated-result repositories do not declare a license in the retrieved metadata; absence of a declaration is not a grant of rights. Full-text papers collected locally are individually classified for public inclusion, with citation/URL records retained for excluded material.

The upstream revision pins, canonical-source hashes and raw semantic fingerprints identify third-party inputs; they do not grant additional rights to those inputs. The acquisition tools fetch canonical data from its pinned upstream source. Joint-analysis source code retains the original-code license scope; cached fitted outputs are research artifacts, not upstream raw redistribution.
